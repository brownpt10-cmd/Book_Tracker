Weekend Trip to Durango

A three-night stake in the San Juans — two parties, one meeting point. Thursday, August 6 through Sunday, August 9, 2026.

SAME FRIENDS, EVERY YEAR

When you want the best kind of reunion, you don't need an invitation — just old friends and a mountain town that knows how to slow things down. It's that simple. This is where the stories get retold (and improved a little every year), where a cold beer tastes better because you're the ones sharing it, and where a good meal turns into three more hours at the table than you planned. We've done this before. We'll do it again. Same friends, same mountains, same easy nights that remind you why you kept these people close all these years.

THE PLAN

Two roads in, one town to meet in.

The Colorado Springs party drives US-160 over Wolf Creek Pass; the Sedona party crosses the high desert and loses an hour to the Arizona-Colorado time gap. Both converge on Ska Brewing, 2:30 PM Thursday.

Three nights downtown, mornings clear and afternoons prone to a mountain storm cell — pack for a 30°F day-to-night swing and budget a buffer for Wolf Creek Pass.

OUTFITTING THE PARTY

What to pack.

His Kit

CLOTHING
  • 3 short-sleeve shirts + 1 lightweight long-sleeve for evenings
  • 2 shorts, 1 pair light pants
  • 3-4 underwear, socks
  • Swim trunks
  • Packable rain jacket or windbreaker
  • Sleepwear, walking shoes + sandals
  • Ball cap, sunglasses
TOILETRIES & EXTRAS
  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+), razor, deodorant, toothbrush/paste
  • Daily meds, phone charger

Her Kit

CLOTHING
  • 3 tops + 1 light layer/cardigan for cool evenings
  • 2 shorts/casual bottoms, 1 sundress or light pants option
  • Underwear, bras, socks for each day
  • 1-2 swimsuits (avoid white — springs will discolor it), cover-up
  • Packable rain layer, sleepwear
  • Walking shoes + sandals, sun hat, sunglasses
TOILETRIES & EXTRAS
  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+), lip balm w/ SPF
  • Skincare/makeup essentials, hair ties/brush, daily meds

Care Items

  • Depends — enough for 4 days (travel day + 3 nights), plus 1-2 spares. The Residence Inn has on-site laundry as backup.
  • Swim diapers — at least 2, for the hot springs. Regular Depends aren't built for water.
