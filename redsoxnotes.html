State of the Sox
  • Boston is 82-70, third in the AL East, 10.5 games behind Tampa Bay (92-59), which has clinched the division.
  • The Yankees (88-64) have also clinched a playoff berth, 4.5 games back of Tampa Bay in the East race.
  • The Red Sox hold the second AL Wild Card with a 5.0-game cushion over the Chicago White Sox, who occupy the third and final spot. Boston's magic number to clinch at least a playoff berth is 5, using Texas (76-76) as the current first team out of a playoff position.
  • Boston has lost 2 straight, both at Texas, and is 5-5 over its last 10 games; the club is 72-53 (.576) since Chad Tracy took over as interim manager on April 26.
  • Ten games remain, starting with tonight's series finale in Arlington, before three at Tampa Bay and a season-ending six-game Fenway homestand against Cleveland and the Cubs.
Contreras Powers Early, Bullpen Stumbles Late
  • Willson Contreras opened the scoring with a 403-foot, two-run homer in the first Wednesday — his 27th of the season and first since returning from the injured list — but Boston's offense went dark behind it, managing just three hits (Contreras, Sogard, Story) and no runs the rest of the way apart from a fifth-inning balk.
  • Jake Bennett kept Boston competitive, allowing three runs on eight hits over 4 2/3 innings, but the bullpen — which had thrown 5 1/3 scoreless innings in Tuesday's loss — cracked in the seventh: Erik Miller hit Corey Seager, walked Jake Burger, then allowed Brandon Nimmo's go-ahead double and a two-run single by Ezequiel Duran.
  • Former Red Sox right-hander Nathan Eovaldi retired the final 11 batters he faced in relief for Texas, and Boston has now been outhit 21-6 through the first two games of the series.
Central Gets Scrambled, West Deadlocked
  • Cleveland has overtaken Chicago atop the AL Central (78-75, a half-game up) since Tuesday, while Houston and Texas are now tied atop the AL West at 76-76 apiece after Wednesday's games.
  • With the division races in flux behind Tampa Bay and the Yankees, the Chicago White Sox (77-75) now occupy the third AL Wild Card spot, with Texas the first team outside a playoff position, 1.0 games back.
  • Toronto, Baltimore, Detroit, Minnesota and Seattle remain mathematically alive for a Wild Card berth, but all trail Boston by at least 5 games with 10 to play.
The Return Timeline
  • Ceddanne Rafaela (right quad strain) is on track to be activated tomorrow, Sept. 18, for the series opener at Tampa Bay — his originally targeted date.
  • Garrett Crochet threw a 30-pitch bullpen session at Fenway Park Tuesday that Tracy called encouraging; the club will decide his next step based on how he responds, with an eye toward a relief role if he's activated for the postseason.
  • Tanner Houck struck out the side in a scoreless inning during his third rehab outing Tuesday at Triple-A Worcester, touching 94.2 mph. He's expected to pitch again Saturday.
  • Tyron Guerrero has been eligible to return since Wednesday but had not been activated as of this issue.
Ward Waiting in the Wings
  • Interim manager Chad Tracy said Wednesday that speedster Braiden Ward "would be considered, for sure" for a September call-up, telling WEEI's "Rich and Ken with Ted Johnson" the club is weighing it.
  • Ward, 27, leads all of Triple-A with 58 stolen bases (269 in his minor-league career) but is hitting a modest .238 with a .712 OPS at Worcester this season; he would need to be added to the 40-man roster.
  • Eli White's foot injury has opened up outfield and pinch-runner space that could make Ward a pure speed option down the stretch or in October.
Playful Nuggets
  • BLOWN LEAD, BAD TIMING: Willson Contreras' first-inning homer had Boston up 2-0 Wednesday, but the Red Sox let it slip away — one of 22 games this season in which they've surrendered a lead and lost, against 36 comeback wins in the other direction.
  • ROAD WARRIORS: Boston's .571 winning percentage on the road (44-33) is nearly 65 points better than its .507 mark at Fenway (38-37), an unusual split for a team built around its home ballpark.
  • GOING TO EXTRAS: The Red Sox are 7-4 in extra-inning games this season, including 5 walk-off wins against just 3 walk-off losses.
  • JULY'S GHOST: Boston's 21-4 July feels like a distant memory after a flat 7-7 September entering Thursday's finale.
  • FOUR LEFT ON THE ROAD: Of Boston's 10 remaining games, only 4 are away from Fenway — tonight and three at Tampa Bay — before a season-ending six-game homestand against Cleveland and the Cubs.
Odds & Ends
  • Clemente nod: Garrett Whitlock is Boston's nominee for the 2026 Roberto Clemente Award, recognized for four years as the Red Sox' Jimmy Fund captain supporting Dana-Farber Cancer Institute patients and families.
  • Bennett's ERA climbs: Jake Bennett's ERA rose to 3.78 after Wednesday's start, his highest mark since early June.
  • Season series on the line: Today's rubber match decides whether Texas takes the six-game season series 4-2 or Boston ties it at 3-3.
  • Worcester winding down: Triple-A Worcester's season ends Sept. 20, continuing to shape Crochet's, Houck's and any Braiden Ward call-up timeline.
