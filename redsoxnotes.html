PÁEZ'S ROUGH DEBUT, QUICK TURNAROUND:
  • Jedixson Páez's first career MLB start lasted 3 2/3 innings Tuesday, and it was an ugly one: 10 hits and 8 earned runs allowed, with 2 walks and 3 strikeouts, as Seattle built an early lead it never relinquished in a 9-6 win.
  • "Struggled a little bit," interim manager Chad Tracy said afterward, noting Seattle's hitters "were definitely on some soft pitches and hit some balls hard" against Páez's changeup- and sweeper-heavy mix.
  • Boston optioned Páez back to Worcester after the game and recalled LHP Raymond Burgos in the corresponding move — the fastest turnaround of any player's stint on this year's active roster.
  • Mickey Gasper (game-tying homer in the first) and pinch-hitter Jahmai Jones (two-run shot in the ninth) kept the deficit from ballooning further, but Boston's comeback bid fell three runs short.
RUTSCHMAN'S ELBOW: MANAGED, NOT ALARMING:
  • Adley Rutschman sat out his second straight game Tuesday with a hyperextended right elbow first injured on a swing Aug. 24 at Miami; Tracy said the issue "could" require monitoring for the rest of the season but stopped short of calling it alarming.
  • Recent imaging showed nothing beyond what the club expected, according to Tracy, and Rutschman played three of four games in last weekend's Yankees series, including a three-hit doubleheader opener, while managing soreness.
  • The three-time All-Star has struggled at the plate since being Boston's headline Aug. 3 Trade Deadline addition, hitting .170 with a .500 OPS across his first 15 games in a Red Sox uniform, but remains valued for his defense and pitching-staff rapport.
  • Tracy said the intent is for Rutschman to return to today's lineup pending a pregame check-in.
GRAY'S SATURDAY PLAN & WORKLOAD WATCH:
  • Sonny Gray's turn in the rotation was pushed back for the second straight time Tuesday, with Tracy targeting Saturday's game at Baltimore (Sep. 5) to get Boston's ace fully rested for the stretch run. "It just was necessary," Tracy said, citing Gray's workload and a rough Aug. 26 outing (7 H, 2 R over 5 IP) as factors.
  • Ranger Suárez has scuffled since returning from the injured list, working to a 5.01 ERA over his last seven outings, and could also be in line for extra rest.
  • Rookies Payton Tolle (131.0 IP) and Jake Bennett (93.2 IP) are both already in uncharted single-season workload territory; Boston could skip a turn or two for either down the stretch.
Odds & Ends
  • Wild Card math: Boston's Bronx series loss last weekend handed the Yankees the season-series tiebreaker, effectively giving New York a five-game cushion in any potential Wild Card seeding tie, per Boston.com's Wednesday analysis.
