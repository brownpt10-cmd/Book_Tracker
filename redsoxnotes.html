State of the Sox
  • Boston is 87-74, third in the AL East and 11.0 games behind champion Tampa Bay (98-63). The Red Sox clinched a postseason berth on Sept. 20 and are locked into the No. 5 seed.
  • Boston plays the No. 4 seed Yankees (93-68) in a best-of-three Wild Card Series at Yankee Stadium, starting Tuesday, Sept. 29. The winner goes to the ALDS against the top-seeded Rays.
  • The Red Sox are 55-28 (.663) since falling to 32-46. Chad Tracy is 77-57 since taking over on April 26, and on Sept. 22 the club dropped his "interim" tag with a three-year contract.
  • Boston has won two straight after sweeping Friday's rain-soaked doubleheader from the Cubs, and is 5-5 over its last 10.
Game 162: Houck's Long Road Back
  • Tanner Houck makes his first big league appearance of 2026 after a season-long recovery from Tommy John surgery. He was activated from the 60-day IL this morning and will work in a bullpen-style role. That gives Tracy a last live look before finalizing the Wild Card roster.
  • To open a 40-man spot for Houck, Eli White moved to the 60-day IL (right foot ligament injury). Rookie Jedixson Páez was optioned to Worcester two days after earning his first MLB win.
  • The finale is a road trip in reverse: Boston flew to St. Petersburg after Friday's doubleheader, had Saturday off, and closes the season at Tropicana Field as the home team. That's the same building where it celebrated its clinch a week ago.
Contreras Back; Gasper Down
  • Willson Contreras bats third at DH today after sitting out both games of Friday's doubleheader. He was hit on the right hand at Texas on Sept. 17. A CT scan showed no fracture, but inflammation and a bone bruise had kept him from swinging for days, and he had publicly doubted he'd be ready for the Wild Card round.
  • Mickey Gasper (.251/.357/.466, 10 HR) felt his right biceps give out on a fifth-inning swing in Game 1 Friday. He went on the 10-day IL today, and Tracy called the injury "significant." He is expected to miss the Wild Card Series.
  • Tyron Guerrero's return lasted less than a week. The right-hander, activated Sept. 20, went back on the 15-day IL (right shoulder strain, retroactive to Sept. 24), costing the bullpen one of its late-inning arms before October.
Bronx Rematch Preview
  • This is the second straight year Boston opens October at Yankee Stadium. In 2025 the Red Sox won Game 1 behind Garrett Crochet and then lost the series in three.
  • Boston started Payton Tolle, Sonny Gray and Ranger Suárez in the Cleveland series (Sept. 22-24), the three starters it plans to use in the Wild Card round. Tracy has not officially announced the order.
  • The Yankees won the 2026 season series 7-6, but Boston took five of the last eight meetings. The Red Sox went 2-4 in the Bronx.
  • NESN reports Yankees manager Aaron Boone said the club was "not planning on" Aaron Judge (calf strain) for the Wild Card round, though Judge had not been officially ruled out.
Playful Nuggets
  • HOME SWEET (ST. PETE) HOME: Boston is 46-35 on the road and 41-39 at home. Its final "home" game of 2026 will be played more than 1,000 miles from Fenway, in the same dome where it clinched a week ago.
  • SLAMMING THE DOOR: The Red Sox are 56-5 this season when they allow two runs or fewer, and have thrown 18 shutouts (suffering only 8).
  • SCORING SHORTAGE: Boston's last five games at Fenway (Sept. 22-25) produced just 16 runs combined: Boston 9, opponents 7. That stretch included back-to-back 1-0 games on Sept. 23 and 24. The Red Sox are 3-2 in 1-0 games this year.
  • COIN-FLIP CLUB: The Red Sox are exactly .500 in one-run games (21-21). By Pythagorean record (687 runs scored, 605 allowed) they "should" be 90-71, three wins better than their actual mark.
  • ROOKIE RELIEF: Jedixson Páez, 22, threw five scoreless innings in relief of Brayan Bello in Friday's nightcap for his first big league win. It's the longest scoreless outing of his brief MLB career.
  • RUN THE TABLE: Friday's doubleheader sweep put Boston 2-0 against the Cubs this season and pushed the Red Sox ahead 15-14 in the all-time interleague series, with one meeting left.
Odds & Ends
  • Front office: Along with Tracy's three-year deal, the club picked up chief baseball officer Craig Breslow's contract options for 2029 and 2030.
  • Crochet watch: Garrett Crochet (left shoulder) hit 96 mph in live BP on Sept. 22 and is viewed as a possible October bullpen option, but remains on the 60-day IL.
  • Final-day scoreboard: Houston and Texas enter Sunday tied at 80-81 for the AL West, with the division, and a Wild Card Series date against the White Sox, still undecided.
  • TV for Tuesday: NESN lists all three Wild Card games on NBC. First-pitch times were still TBD as of this issue.
