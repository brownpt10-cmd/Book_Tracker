State of the Sox

Aaron Judge's Return Raises the Stakes
  • The Yankees activated Aaron Judge from the 60-day IL Monday, reinstating the three-time AL MVP for the first time since May 31 after a stress fracture in his right rib.
  • Judge was hitting .248 with 17 home runs and 38 RBI through 59 games before the injury; New York held its Wild Card position without him for more than three months, and his return arrives without a minor league rehab stint.
  • Boston has trimmed the Yankees' division lead from 9.0 games in late June to 4.0 with Judge now back in the lineup — every remaining game against New York's rivals in the Wild Card race carries added weight.
From Worst to Wild Card: How Boston Got Here
  • As recently as June 24, the Red Sox were 32-46 and coming off a series loss to the last-place Rockies — Garrett Crochet was hurt, Alex Cora had just been fired, and the season looked lost.
  • Since then, Boston has gone 48-20, powered by an unprecedented 21-4 July (best winning percentage since the 1950s) that included a 15-game winning streak from July 3-22, the club's longest of the season.
  • The Aug. 3 trade deadline additions of Adley Rutschman and reliever Erik Miller (13.1 IP, 24 K, sub-1.00 WHIP since arriving) have reinforced a roster already carried by breakout years from Andruw Monasterio, Jahmai Jones and Caleb Durbin.
Tonight's Turn: Bennett Looks to Salvage the Finale
  • Jake Bennett gets the ball for Boston in the rubber match, taking on Angels rookie right-hander Ryan Johnson.
  • Reid Detmers shut Boston down Tuesday, retiring his final 10 batters and striking out nine over six scoreless innings; Zach Neto supplied the offense with a 4-for-5, two-run-homer night.
  • “The slider was very, very difficult to pick up... I just thought he threw the ball really, really well,” interim manager Chad Tracy said of Detmers after the loss.
