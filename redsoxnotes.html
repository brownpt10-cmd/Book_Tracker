Story's Blast Salvages Series Finale
  • Trevor Story's three-run homer in the eighth inning off Jacob Latz erased a 3-1 deficit and lifted Boston to a 4-3 win at Texas on Thursday, helping the Red Sox avoid their first series sweep since July.
  • Sonny Gray worked into the game (roughly 6 1/3 innings, six strikeouts by this issue's own inning tally) but was not involved in the decision; Wyatt Olds picked up the win in relief, and Garrett Whitlock closed it out — issuing a walk around a groundout and a 6-4-3 double play — with Aroldis Chapman (lat tightness) unavailable.
  • Willson Contreras was hit on the right hand by a 92.3 mph sinker in the eighth and left the game; X-rays came back negative, but he called it "sore" and "frustrating" and was held out of Friday's lineup.

Injury Watch: Contreras, Chapman Day to Day
  • Willson Contreras exited Thursday's win after being hit on the right hand; X-rays were negative, but there is bruising and swelling. He was unsure Thursday night whether he'd play this weekend and is out of Friday's lineup.
  • Aroldis Chapman has not pitched since Sept. 13 because of lat tightness and was unavailable to close Thursday. "We're going to be conservative with it," Tracy said. Garrett Whitlock is the primary alternative in the ninth if Chapman is unavailable again.
  • Both are considered day to day entering a series in which Boston could move closer to clinching at least a share of a playoff spot.
The Return Timeline
