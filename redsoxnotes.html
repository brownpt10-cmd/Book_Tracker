State of the Sox
  • Boston is 80-65, third in the AL East, 6.0 games behind Tampa Bay (85-58) and 4.0 behind the Yankees (81-62).
  • The Red Sox hold the second AL Wild Card with a 7.0-game cushion over Cleveland, which holds the third and final Wild Card spot outright. Boston's magic number to clinch a playoff berth is down to 10, per Sports Magic Numbers' live tracker.
  • Boston has won five straight, including Monday's 5-2 series-opening win over the Angels, and hosts Game 2 of that set tonight.
  • Seventeen games remain on the regular-season schedule, including a season-defining set at Tampa Bay (Sep. 18-20) and a season finale against the Cubs at Fenway.
Rutschman's Hot Streak
  • Adley Rutschman has found his stroke after an ice-cold first month in Boston (1-for-14 to start); he's slugging .875 with three home runs over his last four games.
  • His three-run shot off the Pesky Pole was the headline swing of Monday's win over the Angels — his fourth homer as a member of the Red Sox. Boston is 4-0 in games he's caught in September.
  • He's also thrown out 18 would-be base stealers this year between Baltimore and Boston, with a framing run value in the 95th percentile leaguewide.
Bullpen Depth Tested
  • The Red Sox shut down reliever Justin Slaten this week after recurring right elbow inflammation resurfaced during a throwing progression; he hasn't pitched since Aug. 12 and is considered unlikely to return this season.
  • Two other arms are progressing toward possible late-season help: Tanner Houck begins a rehab assignment at Double-A Portland today (a 20-pitch outing, with a second outing planned Friday), and Garrett Crochet has thrown his first bullpen session since a late-May shoulder setback, with another expected soon.
  • Boston's bullpen still ranks among the majors' better relief corps by ERA even after the Slaten setback.
Tonight's Turn: Sandoval Returns to the Rotation
  • Patrick Sandoval starts for the first time in over two weeks, taking on Angels lefty Reid Detmers in Game 2 of the series at Fenway.
  • Los Angeles was eliminated from AL West and Wild Card contention this week, one of only two American League teams (with Oakland) mathematically out with three-plus weeks to play.
  • Boston has taken the first game of each of its last three series and can move to within one win of a series victory tonight.
Playful Nuggets
