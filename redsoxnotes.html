State of the Sox
  • Boston is 65-57 (3rd, AL East, 9.5 GB) after Friday's 8-4 loss to Pittsburgh — the club's sixth defeat in its last seven games after salvaging Thursday's Toronto finale.
  • The Red Sox still hold the AL's second Wild Card spot at 65-57, 5.0 games clear of the cutline (Detroit Tigers, 60-62, hold the third and final spot); the Yankees lead the Wild Card pack at 68-54.
  • Baseball-Reference's playoff odds sit at 97.7% to make the postseason and 9.5% to win the World Series, both drawn directly from the team stats page.
  • Run differential is 542 runs scored against 460 allowed (+82) through Thursday's games, computed directly from the day-by-day log at right — still comfortably the best mark in the AL East on a per-game basis.
  • Boston is in Pittsburgh for a rare interleague set (Game 2 of 3) before returning home to Fenway for three with Arizona starting Monday.
Skid Reaches Six Of Seven In Pittsburgh Opener
  • Friday's series opener at PNC Park got away from Boston early: Rafael Flores Jr. took Jake Bennett deep in the second, and Brandon Lowe's three-run homer off Jovani Morán in the fifth put it out of reach in an 8-4 defeat.
  • Bennett was chased after four innings (5 H, 4 R/4 ER, 6 SO) and took the loss, falling to 7-6; Alec Gamboa turned in three scoreless innings of mop-up relief, one of the few bright spots.
  • The loss dropped Boston to 6-1 in its last seven — sorry, 1-6 — a stretch bookended by the four-game Toronto set (three losses) and now an 0-1 start against the Pirates, with Thursday's 7-0 shutout of the Blue Jays the lone bright spot in between.
Tolle's Gem Salvaged The Toronto Finale
  • Payton Tolle authored the best start of his young career Thursday, throwing eight scoreless innings on just 91 pitches as Boston blanked Toronto 7-0 to avoid a series sweep, improving to 8-6, 2.97 ERA on the year.
  • Caleb Durbin broke the game open with a grand slam in the eighth and added an RBI single in the ninth, finishing 5-for-5 with 5 RBI — his best game of the season.
  • Max Scherzer took the loss for Toronto; Greg Weissert, recalled from Worcester that same day to replace the newly-injured Garrett Whitlock, closed it out with a scoreless ninth.
Rutschman's Rocky Boston Debut
  • Adley Rutschman was activated off the 10-day IL and made his Red Sox debut Aug. 11 in Toronto, going 1-for-3 with two walks in a 3-5 loss; he's cooled since, hitting just .091 (1-for-11) with no extra-base hits over his first three games behind the plate.
  • Jake Rogers was designated for assignment as the corresponding move — he's fully off Boston's 40-man after three brief games in a Red Sox uniform. Connor Wong now backs up Rutschman.
  • Isiah Kiner-Falefa was transferred from the 10-day to the 60-day IL on Aug. 11 (left forearm inflammation), and the Baltimore return package for Rutschman was finalized Aug. 12 when outfield prospect Harold Rivas (Boston's former No. 13 prospect) was sent to the Orioles as the last piece.
  • Tracy has kept Rutschman in the lineup and the 3-hole in each of his first two Pittsburgh-series starts, banking on the switch-hitting catcher's track record to come around.
Bullpen Shuffle: Whitlock To IL, Weissert And Gamboa Up
  • Garrett Whitlock (6-1, 2.05 ERA, 2 SV) was placed on the 15-day IL Aug. 13, retroactive to Aug. 10, with right elbow inflammation; he downplayed any structural concern but has no clear return date.
  • Greg Weissert was recalled from Triple-A Worcester the same day and immediately closed out Thursday's 7-0 win over Toronto with a scoreless ninth.
  • Alec Gamboa was recalled a day earlier (Aug. 12) as the corresponding move for RHP Seth Martinez, who was designated for assignment without ever making his Red Sox or 2026 MLB debut.
Anthony, Story Both Nearing Rehab Assignments
  • Tracy said Thursday that Roman Anthony (wrist, 60-day IL) is closing in on a minor-league rehab assignment as his hitting program has progressed to live velocity work; no firm date was given.
  • Trevor Story (sports hernia surgery, 60-day IL) is also ramping up workouts in Boston with increased intensity, per the same update, though he remains further from an on-field return than Anthony.
  • Neither move is expected before Boston's upcoming homestand at the earliest.
THE 5-RUN RECIPE:
  • Score 5-plus runs and the Red Sox are 47-9 (.839) this season; hold them under 5 and they're 18-48 (.273) — exactly the pattern that played out this week (7-0 win Thursday, 3-run, 4-run and 4-run losses in the other three games).
  • Boston has now thrown 12 shutouts (Thursday's the latest) against 5 suffered, still the widest such margin in the AL East.
ROAD SWEET ROAD, CONTINUED:
  • All four games since the last build were played on the road, and Boston is now 36-26 (.581) away from Fenway against just 29-31 (.483) at home — still the widest reverse home/road split in the AL East.
  • The Red Sox are 5-3 in extra innings and 3-3 in walk-off games this season; none of the last four games needed extra frames. Over the last 10 games Boston is 4-6, a sharp comedown from the 27-of-30 stretch that defined late July.
