Modified Military Diet

The same 3-day plan, same meals, same order — portions adjusted so the protein and calories hold up for a bigger frame. Follow it for 3 days, then eat normally for 4, same as the original. Repeat until you hit your goal weight.

Day 1

Day total: ~1,283 calories · 89g carbs · 50g fat · 123g protein

Breakfast

1/2 grapefruit, 1 slice toast, 2 tbsp peanut butter, 2 eggs, coffee or tea

452 cal | 31g carbs | 27g fat | 23g protein
Lunch

1 cup tuna (in water), 1 slice toast, coffee or tea

235 cal | 12g carbs | 3g fat | 41g protein
Dinner

6 oz any lean meat, 1 cup green beans, 1/2 banana, 1 small apple, 1/2 cup Greek yogurt (in place of ice cream)

596 cal | 46g carbs | 20g fat | 59g protein
