Villa went ahead inside six minutes when Marc Guéhi could not control the ball and McGinn played in Jackson. City dominated the chances, with Zion Suzuki making saves from Ndiaye, Cherki and Gvardiol, and Haaland equalised about seven minutes after the break. Jackson's second, around the hour from Ibrahim Mbaye's cross, slipped under Donnarumma, and Ian Maatsen blocked a late Haaland header. It was Villa's first home league win of the season and City's first league defeat; Maresca: "We deserved something more... sometimes we have to be more lucky." MATCH NOTES

Premier League MW8 — Aston Villa 2-1 Man City
  • Saturday 24 October, Villa Park. Nicolas Jackson scored twice (6' and about 60') as Villa ended City's 100% record; Haaland pulled one back with a header from Ndiaye's cross shortly after the interval.
  • Villa's opener came after Guéhi spilled possession to McGinn; Donnarumma's slow goal-kick had earlier led to a City corner. City had more of the chances but Suzuki, Maatsen and the woodwork denied them.
  • Maresca said City "struggled the first probably 10-15 minutes" and "deserved something more." Dias said the second goal "killed the momentum" after the equaliser. Emery: "Today was a really good test."
  • Villa move to 11 points, ten behind City, after a 0-1-3 start. Matty Cash was booked; no red cards.
Sources: Sky Sports; VAVEL; Irish Examiner; NBC Sports (24-26 Oct 2026).
Premier League MW7 — Man City 3-2 Ipswich Town
  • Saturday 17 October, Etihad. Phil Foden (19') returned from suspension and scored; Daizen Maeda levelled on 21'; Haaland headed City back in front on 34' from Kovačić's cross and added a second on about 68' from Ndiaye's pass.
  • VAR chalked off a Haaland hat-trick goal on 85' for an Enzo Fernández offside in the build-up, then awarded Ipswich a penalty that Zian Flemming converted on 90'.
  • Seventh straight league win to open the season: Maresca is reported as the first manager to win his first seven matches in charge of a club. He called Haaland a leader and captain and said a late knock was "nothing serious."
Sources: Sky Sports; VAVEL (17 Oct 2026); ESPN scoreline. Starting XI and attendance not retrieved.
Champions League MD2 — Man City 2-4 Paris Saint-Germain
  • Wednesday 14 October, Etihad. PSG scored three times in the first 35 minutes: Désiré Doué on the counter (Donnarumma caught at his near post), Ousmane Dembélé with a far-post header, and Vitinha from the edge of the box.
  • Haaland headed City back into it from an Ndiaye cross; Maghnes Akliouche's effort deflected off Donnarumma's hand to restore the margin; Haaland scored again. Exact minutes were not available.
  • Semenyo and Fernández missed clear chances, Nuno Mendes cleared off the line, Haaland hit the bar and Donnarumma saved a late Ferran Torres penalty. Maresca made three half-time substitutions.
  • City now stand 1-0-1 in the league phase after the 2-0 win at Porto.
Sources: Al Jazeera (14 Oct 2026); ESPN scoreline. Direct Maresca quotes were not retrieved.

The Back Line Across Two Very Different Games
  • Against Norwich, Maresca fielded an entirely changed back line: Rico Lewis and Rayan Aït-Nouri at full-back either side of an Abdukodir Khusanov-Vítor Reis centre pairing, protecting debutant goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli to a clean sheet.
  • Aït-Nouri, left out of the derby squad entirely the week before, was directly involved in the opening goal build-up and later teed up Samba's second — a markedly different night to the "tactical omission" storyline of the previous edition.
  • Against Sunderland, the regular back line returned: Matheus Nunes and Joško Gvardiol at full-back, Rúben Dias and Marc Guéhi at centre-back in front of Gianluigi Donnarumma, who was shortlisted for the Premier League's Save of the Month award.
  • Gvardiol supplied the assist for Haaland's winner; Dias headed narrowly wide from a corner and then made a spectacular goal-line clearance from his own heavy touch in a frantic finish.
  • Nico O'Reilly came on for the closing stages against Sunderland (85') and was denied a sixth City goal by goalkeeper Robin Roefs.

SECTION NOTES

The Attack Across Two Contrasting Games
  • Against Norwich, Enzo Maresca rested Erling Haaland entirely and handed the central striking role to 17-year-old academy graduate Floyd Samba, who repaid the trust with a debut brace either side of a Rayan Cherki strike and a first City goal from fellow debutant Allan Elias.
  • Against Sunderland, Haaland returned to a front line featuring Iliman Ndiaye in his first Premier League start of the season — deputising on the left with Foden suspended and Doku only just back from injury — plus Rayan Cherki and Antoine Semenyo, both among the scorers.
  • Enzo Fernández, an unused substitute for the cup tie, was recalled for Sunderland and scored his first City goal to be named man of the match, forming an increasingly productive central pairing with Elliot Anderson.
  • Ayyoub Bouaddi and Mateo Kovačić ran the midfield in the rotated Norwich XI, with Bouaddi's pass to Cherki central to City's second goal; both were unused substitutes three days later against Sunderland.
Sources: mancity.com match reports, 17 & 20 Sep 2026.
