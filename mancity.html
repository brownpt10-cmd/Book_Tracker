Three matches, three wins, City's perfect Premier League start now shares top spot with Arsenal on nine points each — City ahead only on goals scored, seven to six. Erling Haaland's 26th-minute header settled a scrappy 1-0 win over newly promoted Coventry at the Etihad, and doubled as a career landmark: his 300th club goal, reached faster than Kylian Mbappé or Cristiano Ronaldo and behind only Lionel Messi. Deadline-day signing Enzo Fernández was thrust into his debut when Nico O'Reilly pulled up in the warm-up, while Iliman Ndiaye started opposite him in an attacking reshuffle that also saw Marc Guéhi finally revert to centre-back. Attention now turns to Europe: City open their Champions League league-phase campaign at Porto on Tuesday, Enzo Maresca's first Champions League match in charge of the club, before Sunday's derby at Old Trafford.

MATCH NOTES

State of the Club
  • City sit top of the Premier League after matchweek 3, level on nine points with Arsenal but ahead on goals scored, seven to six, and on goal difference, +5 to +5 split only by the scored-goals tiebreak.
  • Hull City, newly promoted, sit third on seven points without conceding a goal in three matches.
  • The Maresca era is now 3-0-1 across all competitions: the Community Shield defeat to Arsenal, then league wins over Bournemouth, Crystal Palace and Coventry.
  • Tuesday's trip to Porto is City's first competitive match outside England this season and the first of eight Champions League league-phase fixtures.
Table position and points per worldfootball.net's Premier League standings, updated through matches played 4–6 September 2026.
Matchweek 3 Recap — City 1-0 Coventry
  • Saturday 5 September, Etihad Stadium. Erling Haaland headed home Antoine Semenyo's cross on 26 minutes, in a move Enzo Fernández helped start on his full debut — the only goal of the game.
  • Coventry, without a goal in three league matches, defended deep and finished with just 22% possession, but substitutes Loum Tchaouna and Ellis Simms both had late half-chances as City were left to see out a nervy final stretch.
  • Nico O'Reilly, originally named in the XI at left-back, was withdrawn during the warm-up with a back problem; Fernández came into midfield alongside Elliot Anderson, with Marc Guéhi moving back to centre-back and Iliman Ndiaye starting on the left in place of Phil Foden.
  • Maresca: "It was a little bit of an ugly win. It's something we need and a clean sheet is important." Haaland: "It was a very good [game]. Started with a win, so couldn't get any better."
Sources: Sky Sports and ESPN match reports, 5 Sep 2026; Sports Mole on the O'Reilly warm-up injury and Fernández's late promotion to the XI.
Storyline of the Week — Haaland's 300th
  • Haaland's header at Coventry was the 300th club goal of his career: 165 for City, 86 for Borussia Dortmund, 29 for Red Bull Salzburg and 20 for Molde.
  • He reached the mark in 384 appearances — quicker than Kylian Mbappé and Cristiano Ronaldo needed, and second only to Lionel Messi's 365 games.
  • It was also his 20th headed Premier League goal for City, moving him clear of Sergio Agüero's club record for headers in the competition.
  • City's goal came from an Enzo Fernández pass into Semenyo, whose cross Haaland converted — a full debut involvement to remember for the £125m signing.
Sources: Sports Mole "Erling Haaland breaks new Sergio Aguero record with 300th career goal vs. Coventry"; ESPN "Haaland outpaces Mbappé to 300th club goal," both 5–6 Sep 2026.
Continental Bow — Porto Awaits
  • City's Champions League league phase runs to eight matches: home to PSG, Sporting CP, Napoli and AEK Athens; away to Barcelona, Porto, RB Leipzig and Lens.
  • Tuesday's trip to Porto is Enzo Maresca's first Champions League match as City manager. His most recent European work came at Chelsea, where he won the 2024-25 Conference League and went 3-1-2 across six Champions League matches.
  • Porto, under Francesco Farioli, arrive on the back of seven straight wins across all competitions per pre-match previews, with their defence conceding only once in six games.
  • Doku's continued absence and the fresh O'Reilly and Ndiaye knocks leave City's front-line and left-side options stretched three days after the Coventry win.
Sources: mancity.com Champions League fixture confirmation (carried from 3 Sep build); Goal.com and Sports Mole Porto preview pieces, 7 Sep 2026; Maresca's Chelsea European record as reported in UK match-preview coverage.

What Changed in Matchweek 3
  • The projected reshuffle from last week's build arrived in full: Marc Guéhi moved back to centre-back alongside Rúben Dias, with Abdukodir Khusanov at right-back and Joško Gvardiol at left-back.
  • That left-back slot was only needed because Nico O'Reilly, originally named to start there, felt his back in the warm-up and was withdrawn — Enzo Fernández came into central midfield instead, not at full-back, alongside Elliot Anderson.
  • Gianluigi Donnarumma kept a clean sheet, his and City's first of the season, behind a back line that limited Coventry to 22% possession and few clear chances.
  • Rúben Dias again wore the armband and played the full 90, his third of the season, ahead of Maresca's four-strong leadership group announcement on Friday.
