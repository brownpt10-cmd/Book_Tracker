Fifth edition of the season — first Champions League result banked. Since the 7 September edition, City won 2-0 away at FC Porto in their Champions League league-phase opener, Erling Haaland scoring both goals either side of a full City debut start for 18-year-old Ayyoub Bouaddi. Rayan Aït-Nouri also made his first appearance of the season off the bench and set up Haaland's second, while Iliman Ndiaye came through 62 minutes as a substitute with no sign of the knock that ended his Coventry debut early. This build recaps Porto and turns to Sunday's Manchester derby at Old Trafford, where City's 100% start to the league season is on the line against Michael Carrick's Manchester United. See Availability Watch below for Doku and O'Reilly's latest status.

Enzo Maresca's first Champions League match in charge ended the way his three Premier League games have: a City win, this time 2-0 away at FC Porto in a loud, occasionally tense Estádio do Dragão. Erling Haaland settled a goalless first half with a 47th-minute header from Enzo Fernández's cross, then added a stoppage-time second from Rayan Aït-Nouri's cutback after both had come off the bench. The headline story, though, was 18-year-old Ayyoub Bouaddi's full City debut start in central midfield — an assured, unbothered 75 minutes that had both Haaland and Maresca reaching for superlatives afterward. City finished with 60% possession, 18 shots to Porto's four and nine on target to none. Attention now turns to Sunday: City's 100% Premier League start is on the line at Old Trafford, where Michael Carrick's Manchester United — 11th after three matches — await.

Champions League MD1 — FC Porto 0-2 Man City
  • Tuesday 8 September, Estádio do Dragão. Erling Haaland headed home Enzo Fernández's cross on 47 minutes, then converted Rayan Aït-Nouri's stoppage-time cutback for 2-0 (90+1') — his 36th Champions League goal for City, level with Sergio Agüero's club record.
  • City dominated the underlying numbers: 60% possession, 18 shots to Porto's four, nine on target to zero, 583 passes completed at 92% accuracy.
  • The match turned tense in the first half after a clash between Marc Guéhi and Porto's Gabri Veiga drew in security staff before calm was restored; no cards of note followed for City.
  • Maresca: "I think we could've scored more goals, especially in the first half, but overall I think we deserved to win the game... We created enough chances to win the game. We didn't concede a lot." He added that thoughts would turn to the derby once his players had rested.
Sources: mancity.com and Sky Sports match reports, 8 Sep 2026; theanalyst.com/Opta Analyst stats piece; RTÉ Sport and Al Jazeera on the Guéhi-Veiga incident and Haaland's double.
Storyline of the Week — Bouaddi's Arrival
  • Ayyoub Bouaddi, an unused substitute in each of his first two matchday squads, was handed a surprise full start in central midfield at a hostile Porto — Enzo Maresca's first selection call of the kind since the teenager's £86m arrival from Lille.
  • The 18-year-old completed 71 touches at 95% pass accuracy and won every tackle he contested across 75 composed minutes before making way.
  • Haaland afterward: "We've got a gem" — one of several glowing notices for Bouaddi from City's dressing room and press coverage this week.
  • His introduction pushed Elliot Anderson and Mateo Kovačić back to the bench, sharpening the midfield rotation picture ahead of the derby.
Sources: mancity.com "We've got a gem in Bouaddi, says Haaland" (8 Sep 2026); Yardbarker "Erling Haaland and Enzo Maresca salute 'gem' Ayyoub Bouaddi..."; Sports Mole lineup coverage.
