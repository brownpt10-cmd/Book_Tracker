City's opening weekend answered one question and left the bigger one open. The question it answered: this squad, stripped of Rodri, Bernardo Silva and John Stones inside three months, can still find a way to win a match it was losing with six minutes left. The question it left open: what the midfield actually is. Maresca's answer on Sunday was to push Marc Guéhi — a centre-half — into the middle of it, and the England defender repaid him with the equaliser and, per his manager, "the complete performance." Selhurst Park is where that experiment meets its first away test, and it does so against the club Guéhi captained until January. Palace arrive beaten and goalless from Goodison's replacement under a manager, Pierre Sage, who has yet to take a competitive point. City have not lost at Selhurst Park since April 2015.

MATCH NOTES

State of the Club
  • City sit 8th after matchweek 1 on 3 points and a +1 goal difference — level on points, goal difference and goals scored with Ipswich Town in 7th.
  • Brighton lead the table on a 4-0 opening win; Arsenal and Brentford follow on +3. Aston Villa are bottom on −4.
  • The Maresca era is 1-0-1 across all competitions: beaten 3-0 by Arsenal in the Community Shield, then 2-1 winners over Bournemouth.
  • Friday is City's first Premier League away fixture of the campaign, and the last match before the transfer window closes on 31 August.
Official Premier League table via mancity.com/tables, last updated 25 August 2026. Positions 7 and 8 are separated by a tiebreak beyond points, goal difference and goals scored; the page reports the official ordering rather than inferring which criterion split them.
Matchweek 1 Recap — City 2-1 Bournemouth
  • 23 August, Etihad Stadium, attendance 60,645. Marcus Tavernier put Bournemouth ahead on 26 minutes, finishing an Evanilson cross at the back post.
  • Marc Guéhi headed home Rayan Cherki's corner on 84; Joško Gvardiol finished Cherki's through ball a minute into stoppage time. The winner was flagged offside and awarded after VAR.
  • City had the better of it from the first whistle — Petrovic denied Rico Lewis inside five minutes, and Haaland missed the game's clearest chance on 14 after Nico O'Reilly's cross.
  • Maresca: "The spirit was fantastic. It's a game we deserved to win… Second half their only shot was on 80 minutes so I think we deserved it."
  • Maresca again, on the contrast with the Shield defeat: "I feel the same as after Arsenal. You judge because of the results, it's normal, but I don't see a difference. This is a work in progress."
Source: mancity.com match report, Neil Leigh, 23 Aug 2026, 16:00 BST. Quotes reproduced as published, including the club's own bracketed insertion.
Ten Days to Deadline — Storyline of the Week
  • The summer window closes on 31 August 2026. City have sold two attacking players in the past week and have not yet replaced Rodri with a specialist holding midfielder.
  • Savinho joined Tottenham Hotspur permanently on 25 August after 84 appearances and seven goals in two seasons. Maresca said the winger had asked to leave "from day one."
  • Omar Marmoush is the next name in the queue — reporting has Spurs in advanced talks and terms agreed with the player, with the clubs still apart on fee.
  • Maresca on Marmoush: "I don't know. Omar is still here, and he is still with us. He is different from Savinho, so we will see what happens."
  • Maresca on the plan: "We don't have a minimum or maximum number of signings, it depends on certain factors. We know exactly what we need… We have 10 days ahead of us, and we hope we can carry out what we have planned."
Sources: mancity.com "Savinho signs for Spurs" (Jack Mumford, 25 Aug 2026, 10:00); Maresca press-conference quotes as reported by Goal.com and Sports Mole (Aug 2026). The Guéhi Homecoming
  • Guéhi joined City from Crystal Palace in January 2026 for a reported £20m on a five-and-a-half-year deal. Friday is his first return to Selhurst Park in sky blue.
  • He was used in central midfield against Bournemouth, not at centre-back — Maresca's stopgap answer to the Rodri vacancy — and scored the equaliser from a corner.
  • Maresca's verdict, per the club: Guéhi "put in the complete performance."
  • It is the second consecutive matchweek in which a City forward or defender has faced a former employer: Antoine Semenyo met Bournemouth on Sunday, Guéhi meets Palace on Friday.
Sources: mancity.com match report and post-match interview billing (23 Aug 2026); January 2026 transfer terms as reported at the time and carried in this page's previous build. City do not disclose transfer fees, so the £20m figure is a media figure.

  • Elliot Anderson — withdrawn on 63 minutes against Bournemouth with cramp, not a hamstring problem. Available.

  • Erling Haaland and several late World Cup returnees are reported as not yet at full match sharpness rather than injured.
  • Suspensions: none. City picked up no cards of consequence in matchweek 1 and carry a clean disciplinary slate to Selhurst Park.

The Selhurst Split
  • City have won 20 of 32 meetings with Palace, drawn eight, and scored 70 goals — a 62.5% win rate across the fixture.
  • City's last defeat at Selhurst Park came in April 2015. That is eleven and a half seasons without losing there.
  • Haaland has scored eight goals against Palace, more than any other player in City's history.
  • The most recent meeting at Selhurst was a 3-0 City win on 14 December 2025 — Haaland twice, Foden once.
  • The caveat: Palace beat City in the 2025 FA Cup final, so this is not a fixture City have simply owned in one-off conditions.

What Changed in Matchweek 1
  • Maresca's back line against Bournemouth was Khusanov, Dias, Guéhi and Gvardiol, with Rico Lewis at right-back and Nico O'Reilly on the left — a different shape to the Community Shield, where Khusanov played right-back.
  • Guéhi did not stay in it. He was pushed into central midfield and finished the match as City's equalising goalscorer.
  • City conceded once, from a cross Evanilson delivered to the back post after Bournemouth broke down the right. It is the only shot on target Bournemouth managed after the interval, per Maresca, who put their second-half tally at one shot on 80 minutes.
  • Rúben Dias wore the armband again with Rodri and Bernardo Silva gone.
