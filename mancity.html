The Back Line Across Two Very Different Games
  • Against Norwich, Maresca fielded an entirely changed back line: Rico Lewis and Rayan Aït-Nouri at full-back either side of an Abdukodir Khusanov-Vítor Reis centre pairing, protecting debutant goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli to a clean sheet.
  • Aït-Nouri, left out of the derby squad entirely the week before, was directly involved in the opening goal build-up and later teed up Samba's second — a markedly different night to the "tactical omission" storyline of the previous edition.
  • Against Sunderland, the regular back line returned: Matheus Nunes and Joško Gvardiol at full-back, Rúben Dias and Marc Guéhi at centre-back in front of Gianluigi Donnarumma, who was shortlisted for the Premier League's Save of the Month award.
  • Gvardiol supplied the assist for Haaland's winner; Dias headed narrowly wide from a corner and then made a spectacular goal-line clearance from his own heavy touch in a frantic finish.
  • Nico O'Reilly came on for the closing stages against Sunderland (85') and was denied a sixth City goal by goalkeeper Robin Roefs.

SECTION NOTES

The Attack Across Two Contrasting Games
  • Against Norwich, Enzo Maresca rested Erling Haaland entirely and handed the central striking role to 17-year-old academy graduate Floyd Samba, who repaid the trust with a debut brace either side of a Rayan Cherki strike and a first City goal from fellow debutant Allan Elias.
  • Against Sunderland, Haaland returned to a front line featuring Iliman Ndiaye in his first Premier League start of the season — deputising on the left with Foden suspended and Doku only just back from injury — plus Rayan Cherki and Antoine Semenyo, both among the scorers.
  • Enzo Fernández, an unused substitute for the cup tie, was recalled for Sunderland and scored his first City goal to be named man of the match, forming an increasingly productive central pairing with Elliot Anderson.
  • Ayyoub Bouaddi and Mateo Kovačić ran the midfield in the rotated Norwich XI, with Bouaddi's pass to Cherki central to City's second goal; both were unused substitutes three days later against Sunderland.
Sources: mancity.com match reports, 17 & 20 Sep 2026.
