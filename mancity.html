Seventh edition of the season — two wins banked, perfect league record intact. Since the 16 September edition, City beat Norwich City 5-0 in the Carabao Cup third round, with 17-year-old academy graduate Floyd Samba scoring a dream debut brace and fellow debutant Allan Elias also on target, then won an eight-goal Etihad thriller 5-3 against Sunderland to stay top of the Premier League — the division's only side with a 100% record through five matches. Jérémy Doku made his first appearance since the Community Shield as a substitute in that game. The squad has since scattered for the September/October international window, headlined by Erling Haaland's match-winning brace for Norway. There is no City fixture this week; this build recaps both wins in full and looks ahead to the return at Anfield on 11 October. City eased into the Carabao Cup fourth round with a comfortable 5-0 win over Championship side Norwich City, handing full debuts to goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli, academy striker Floyd Samba and Brazilian winger Allan Elias in a heavily rotated XI — Enzo Maresca made ten changes from the derby win at Old Trafford. Samba's poise on his first senior start stole the headlines, with a 29th-minute header and a brilliant curled finish early in the second half either side of goals from Rayan Cherki and the debutant Allan; substitute Ryan McAidoo added a fifth in injury time. Three days later, a much-changed Sunderland side proved far tougher opposition at the Etihad, twice pegging City back through a Brian Brobbey hat-trick before Erling Haaland's late header settled an eight-goal thriller and kept City's 100% Premier League record intact — the division's only unbeaten, undrawn side after five rounds. Enzo Maresca called it the toughest game of the season so far, and said afterwards he "would prefer to win 1-0" than ship three goals in a frantic, end-to-end contest. The squad has now dispersed for the September/October international window; City do not play again until the trip to Anfield on 11 October.

Carabao Cup R3 — Man City 5-0 Norwich City
  • Thursday 17 September, Etihad Stadium. Enzo Maresca made ten changes from the derby win at Old Trafford, handing full debuts to Gerónimo Rulli, Floyd Samba and Allan Elias, with Erling Haaland left out of the squad entirely.
  • Samba, deployed as a makeshift striker, headed City in front in the 29th minute from a cross diverted into his path by Allan Elias, then curled in a brilliant second on 54 minutes after Rayan Aït-Nouri picked him out on the right of the box.
  • Rayan Cherki doubled the lead on 32 minutes, powering onto an Ayyoub Bouaddi pass before drilling low past former City keeper Daniel Grimshaw; Allan Elias marked his own debut three minutes into the second half with a low finish from the left of the box.
  • Substitute Ryan McAidoo rounded off the scoring a minute into injury time. Rulli was untroubled behind a Khusanov-Reis centre-back pairing and completed a clean-sheet debut. Attendance: 32,451.
  • Maresca on Samba afterwards: "I am satisfied not just for goals — for the entire performance he was good... He's very good [at] finishing and the actions, but they are young and we need to go slowly."
  • The win stretched City's run to a sixth straight victory in all competitions and their last five games have come by an aggregate score of 23-1, per mancity.com. Reward: a fourth-round home tie against Brighton, since confirmed for Wednesday 28 October.
Sources: mancity.com "Debut delight for Samba and Allan as City power past Norwich" match report, 17 Sep 2026; Sky Sports "Man City 5-0 Norwich: Floyd Samba enjoys dream debut with double" (17 Sep 2026); ESPN game analysis.
Premier League MW5 — Man City 5-3 Sunderland
  • Sunday 20 September, Etihad Stadium, attendance 60,312. Enzo Fernández put City ahead in the 9th minute with his first goal for the club, a near-post strike as Sunderland's defence anticipated a City free-kick into the box; Brian Brobbey levelled three minutes later.
  • Rayan Cherki restored the lead on 29 minutes, finishing low after a City press forced a Sunderland turnover; Brobbey again equalised within minutes (33'), the goal standing after a lengthy VAR check confirmed he was just onside.
  • Antoine Semenyo struck twice either side of the interval (43', 57') to put City two goals clear, but Brobbey completed his hat-trick on 59 minutes to make it 4-3 and keep Sunderland in the game.
  • With six minutes left, Erling Haaland turned in a Joško Gvardiol cross for the winner, the goal standing on review; the finish means Haaland has now scored against all 25 clubs he has faced in the Premier League, taking his tally to seven goals in seven appearances this season.
  • Jérémy Doku came on (75') for his first minutes since the Community Shield; Nico O'Reilly was denied a sixth by goalkeeper Robin Roefs, and Sunderland's Dan Ballard hit the post late on.
  • Maresca post-match: "To be honest, I would prefer to win 1-0 than 5-3, but I expected a very difficult game... We found a way to win the game." Man of the match Antoine Semenyo added: "We knew it would be hard. They're a very good team and they made it difficult for us in so many periods of the game."
Sources: mancity.com "City win eight-goal thriller to move clear at the top of the Premier League" match report and Maresca/Semenyo post-match reaction pieces, 20 Sep 2026; Sky Sports and ESPN match reports and stats.

The Back Line Across Two Very Different Games
  • Against Norwich, Maresca fielded an entirely changed back line: Rico Lewis and Rayan Aït-Nouri at full-back either side of an Abdukodir Khusanov-Vítor Reis centre pairing, protecting debutant goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli to a clean sheet.
