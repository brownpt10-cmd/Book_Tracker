Insulin-Conscious Design

Every meal pairs carbs with protein and fat rather than standing alone. Rice, tortillas and other starches are capped around 1/2 cup per plate so no single meal carries a big carb bolus. On fasting days, Meal 1 leads with eggs, avocado and protein — carbs stay under 10g — so breaking the 16-hour fast doesn't open with a spike stacked on top of it.

Fasting-Day Window — Thu · Sat · Sun

16:8, no oatmeal. Three meals about 2h45m apart inside the 8-hour window. Meal 1 leads with protein and fat, not carbs, so breaking the fast doesn't spike insulin on top of the long fasted stretch.
