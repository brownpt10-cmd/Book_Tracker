DEADLINE: MONDAY, AUG. 3 · 6:00 PM ET — ROUGHLY 48 HOURS AWAY

2026 Trade Deadline — The Tigers Have Tipped Toward Selling

The picture has flipped hard since our last refresh on 7/25. Detroit is now 52-58, 4th in the AL Central and roughly 5.5 games back of first place — still just 3.5 games from the final AL Wild Card spot, but with four teams ahead of them in that chase. The Tigers lost 4 of 5 in a brutal stretch capped by blowing a 7-0 lead to the Orioles on 7/29, then bounced back with a 13-1 rout of the A's on 7/31. In between, Detroit scratched trade candidate Casey Mize from his Friday start — a move MLB.com and beat reporters widely read as a signal the club plans to sell rather than protect an active roster piece. The first domino already fell: catcher Jake Rogers was designated for assignment and dealt to Baltimore for catching prospect Zane Barnhart earlier this week.

The headline story is Tarik Skubal. ESPN's Jeff Passan reported the Tigers have decided to trade their ace and that a deal "could come soon," but USA Today's Bob Nightengale reported rival executives said Detroit hadn't seriously engaged in talks as of Saturday morning, and MLB Network's Jon Paul Morosi described the current state as preliminary prospect-name conversations to see which club can meet Detroit's price. In other words: directionally selling, but nothing imminent as of this snapshot. Given that lean, the buy-side needs matrix below (a right-handed bat, bullpen help, corner-infield/SS and young-pitching depth) now looks like a much lower-probability path for Detroit — it's kept here for reference, but the far more live storylines are the Skubal sell-side section and the list of other Tigers pieces that could move, both further down the page.
